Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy covers the KwekKwek & Lala Publisher ("the app"), an internal tool that publishes our own videos to our own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts.

What the app accesses

  • Basic profile information of our own connected accounts (account id, name, avatar).
  • Statistics of our own accounts and videos (views, likes, followers), to see which videos work.
  • Permission to upload videos to our own accounts.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect data about viewers or other users.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.
  • It does not use data for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on our own computer, encrypted where the software supports it, and are never published. Disconnecting an account in the app, or revoking access in the platform's settings, deletes the token.

Deletion

Because only our own accounts are connected, there is no third-party data to delete. You can ask about this policy through any KwekKwek & Lala account.
